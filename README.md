# CancerPred: Analysis and prediction of cancerlectins using evolutionary and domain information

**CancerPred** is a specialized computational resource developed for the identification and analysis of CancerPreds—lectins that play significant roles in tumor cell differentiation,
cell-cell interaction, and metastasis. This platform provides the first computational method to distinguish CancerPreds from a large pool of non-cancer-related lectins using evolutionary and domain information.

**Web Server:** https://webs.iiitd.edu.in/raghava/cancer_pred/

## About the Research

Lectins are proteins that specifically recognize and bind to carbohydrate moieties on proteins or lipids. CancerPreds are a subset of these proteins involved in various stages of cancer progression.
Due to the rapid accumulation of protein sequences with unknown functions in the post-genomic era, this tool was developed to automate the identification of lectins with oncogenic potential.

* **Dataset:** The models were built on a non-redundant dataset of **Cancerlectines** and **non-Cancerlectins**.


* **Methodology:** The platform utilizes Support Vector Machine (SVM) models based on various protein features, including amino acid composition and evolutionary information (PSSM profiles).



## Key Features

### 1. Robust Predictive Models

* **Composition-Based Prediction:** Differentiates proteins based on amino acid preferences, noting that residues like Leucine and Proline are more frequent in Cancerlectins.


* **Evolutionary Information:** Uses **Position-Specific Scoring Matrices (PSSM)** generated by PSI-BLAST to capture conserved evolutionary patterns.


* **Domain Identification:** Leverages **Pfam domain** information, which achieved the highest individual accuracy of 75.31%.


* **Hybrid Approach:** Combines amino acid composition and PSSM profiles to achieve a maximum accuracy of **77.13%**.



### 2. Comparative Analysis

* **BLAST Search:** An integrated similarity search tool that achieved 43.25% accuracy in distinguishing the two classes.


* **Amino Acid Propensity:** Detailed analysis of which residues are preferred or avoided in cancer-related vs. normal lectins.



### 3. Integrated Web-Bench

* **Prediction Module:** Submit protein sequences to predict their status as Cancerlectins or non-Cancerlectins.


* **Search & Browse:** Facility to explore the dataset based on protein names, source organisms, and functional domains.



## Applications

* **Functional Annotation:** Identifying the specific functions of newly discovered lectin sequences.


* **Cancer Research:** Understanding the role of carbohydrate-binding proteins in tumor metastasis and cell signaling.


* **Therapeutic Targeting:** Highlighting potential cancer-related lectins for further experimental validation as drug targets.
